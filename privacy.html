What We Collect

Information you give us

The quote form on our contact page asks for your name, phone number, email address, vehicle year, make and model, insurance carrier, and a description of the damage. You may include a claim number. If you call, email, or send damage photos, we keep what you send.

Information collected automatically

Our host records standard web server data: IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, and the page that referred you. We use it to keep the site running and to see which pages people read.

What we never ask for online

Do not send Social Security numbers, driver's license images, or payment card numbers through this website. We do not request them here and cannot secure them in transit.

Why We Collect It

  • Write your estimate and schedule the repair.
  • Order the correct parts for your vehicle.
  • Communicate with your insurance carrier about your claim.
  • Reach you with repair updates and completion notice.
  • Keep the repair file that backs your workmanship warranty.
  • Meet tax, accounting, and recordkeeping obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not build profiles of you across other websites.

Who Sees It

  • Mike Knight Customs staff working on your vehicle or claim.
  • Your insurance carrier and its adjuster, for your claim only.
  • Parts suppliers, limited to what an order requires.
  • The service providers listed below, limited to their function.
  • Law enforcement or courts, where the law requires it.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are used in the California Consumer Privacy Act.

Cookies and Tracking

This website sets no cookies of its own and stores nothing in your browser. Our traffic analytics are cookieless and do not follow you to other sites.

The Google Map embedded on the contact page is loaded from Google and may set its own cookies once it appears. Blocking third-party cookies in your browser stops that without breaking the rest of the site.

How Long We Keep It

Repair files, estimates, and claim correspondence stay on record for as long as your workmanship warranty runs and as long as tax and recordkeeping law requires. A quote request that never becomes a repair is kept only as a business record and is deleted on request.

California Privacy Rights

California residents have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act.

  • Know. Ask what personal information we hold about you and where it came from.
  • Delete. Ask us to delete it, subject to records we must keep by law.
  • Correct. Ask us to fix information that is wrong.
  • Opt out of sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of.
  • Non-discrimination. Exercising a right will never change your price or the quality of your repair.

To exercise any of these, call (530) 214-9949 or email mikeknightcustoms@hotmail.com with the vehicle and approximate date so we can find your file. We will verify your identity before acting, and we respond within the time the statute allows.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS, and access to repair files is limited to staff who need them. No transmission over the internet is completely secure. Send sensitive documents in person or by phone rather than through this website.

Children

This site is meant for vehicle owners and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes and Contact

When this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Material changes will be described here rather than made quietly.
